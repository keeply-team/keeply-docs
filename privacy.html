Keeply Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 10, 2025

Keeply ("Bot", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any information that may be processed while using the bot through Discord.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, how it is protected, and your rights regarding your data when interacting with Keeply.

1. Information We Collect

Keeply is designed to minimize data collection and only processes information necessary for its functionality.

  • Discord User Information: User IDs, usernames, and display names may be accessed through Discord APIs when required for bot functionality.
  • Commands and Interactions: Commands executed through the bot may be temporarily processed to generate responses and provide requested services.
  • User Content: Information intentionally submitted by users, such as notes or saved content, may be stored in order to provide the bot's features.
  • Technical Information: Basic operational logs may be generated for troubleshooting, security, and performance monitoring.

2. How We Use Information

The information processed by Keeply is used solely to:

  • Provide and operate bot features.
  • Store and retrieve user-created content.
  • Maintain service stability and reliability.
  • Prevent abuse, spam, and malicious activity.
  • Improve existing features and user experience.

3. Data Storage

Some information submitted through Keeply may be stored securely in databases or storage systems controlled by the Keeply Team.

Stored information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the requested functionality or comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Sharing

Keeply does not sell, rent, trade, or share user information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Information may only be disclosed when:

  • Required by applicable law or legal process.
  • Necessary to protect the security or integrity of the service.
  • Required to investigate abuse, fraud, or violations of these policies.

5. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are implemented to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

6. User Rights

Users may request information regarding data associated with their use of Keeply.

Where applicable, users may also request correction or deletion of stored information, subject to technical limitations and legal requirements.

7. Children's Privacy

Keeply is not directed toward children under the minimum age required by Discord's Terms of Service.

The bot does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Third-Party Services

Keeply operates through Discord and relies on Discord's platform and APIs.

Users are also subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service when using the bot.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Keeply Team reserves the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time.

Any updates will be reflected by revising the "Last Updated" date shown at the top of this page.
